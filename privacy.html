1.About Clipponak

Clipponak is a self-hosted content tool. It runs on its operator's own computer and turns long videos that the operator owns, or has permission to use, into short vertical clips. After the account owner authorizes it, Clipponak publishes those clips to their own TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram accounts through each platform's official API. Clipponak is not a hosted service and has no user sign-up.

2.Information we access

Clipponak only accesses data that the account owner explicitly authorizes on the platform's own login screen:

PlatformPermissionData accessed
TikTokuser.info.basicOpen ID, display name, and avatar, to show which account is connected.
TikTokvideo.publishCreator settings needed to post (such as allowed privacy levels), and permission to upload videos that the account owner approved in Clipponak.
TikTokvideo.listPublic metrics (views, likes, comments, shares) of videos that Clipponak published.
YouTubeupload, read-onlyChannel name; uploading approved videos; view, like, and comment counts of those videos.
Instagrambasic, content publish, insightsUsername and account ID; publishing approved Reels; insights (views, likes, comments, shares, saves) of those Reels.

Clipponak never receives or stores your social media passwords. Login happens on each platform's official page; Clipponak only receives an access token.

To choose clips, Clipponak also processes the source videos you add: their audio is transcribed on your computer, and the transcript text is sent to the Google Gemini API for analysis.

3.How information is used

  • To publish videos that the account owner reviewed and approved, at the time they scheduled.
  • To show the connected account name and the status of each post.
  • To show post performance in Clipponak's local analytics dashboard.

Clipponak does not use platform data for advertising, profiling, or selling, and does not post anything that the account owner has not approved.

4.Where information is stored

All data, including access tokens, post history, statistics, videos, and transcripts, is stored locally on the computer running Clipponak. Clipponak has no central server and no database outside that computer.

5.Sharing and third parties

Clipponak does not sell, rent, or share personal data. Data leaves the computer only to reach the services needed to do the job:

  • TikTok, YouTube (Google), and Instagram (Meta), to publish videos and read their metrics.
  • Google Gemini API, which receives transcript text of source videos (not platform account data) to select clips.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy.

6.Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. Clicking Disconnect on Clipponak's Accounts page deletes the stored token immediately.
  • You can also revoke access at any time from each platform's app permission settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).
  • Post history and statistics stay on the local computer until deleted by the operator. To request deletion, contact us at the address below.

7.Security

Tokens are stored only on the operator's computer, are never displayed in the interface or logs, and are sent only to the official API of the platform they belong to. Login requests are protected against forgery with a single-use state value.

8.Children

Clipponak is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.
